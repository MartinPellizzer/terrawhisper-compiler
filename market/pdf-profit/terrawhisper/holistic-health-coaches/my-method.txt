**Method:** Medicinal Herbal Tinctures for Holistic Health Coaches

**Mission:** Provide a turn-key solution for Holistic Health Coaches to easily and confidently create effective yet affordable herbal products that meet the ever-changing regulations and laws, without worrying about complex laws and regulations.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Initial Consultation** (30 minutes to 1 hour):
	* Discuss client goals, target market, and product requirements.
	* Identify key herbal ingredients and their intended uses.
2. **Herbal Ingredient Selection and Sourcing** (1-3 days):
	* Use a pre-verified library of high-quality suppliers to source herbs.
	* Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), FDA regulations).
3. **Tincture Formula Creation** (1-2 days):
	* Utilize a proprietary herbal formula library to create custom blends.
	* Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., labeling, packaging).
4. **Tincture Production** (2-5 days):
	* Use a pre-verified library of high-quality suppliers to source tincture ingredients.
	* Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., GMP, FDA regulations).
5. **Quality Control and Testing** (1-3 days):
	* Conduct quality control and testing to ensure product efficacy and safety.
	* Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements (e.g., labeling, packaging).
6. **Product Packaging and Labeling** (1-2 days):
	* Use pre-approved labels and packaging materials to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. **Product Launch and Marketing** (1-3 days):
	* Develop marketing materials and launch the product.
